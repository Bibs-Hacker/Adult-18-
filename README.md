## 💫 Overview

**Neon Love Vault** is a password-protected digital safe crafted for those who mix technology with feeling.  
It blends **romantic aesthetics**, **neon animation**, and **secure logic** to create a private zone for your heartfelt content — from notes to cherished media.

---

## 🌹 Core Features

- 🔒 **Password Access Portal** — keeps everything safe and personal  
- 💖 **Romantic Neon Glow UI** — pink, violet, and cyan gradient fusion  
- 🌙 **Soft Animations** — gentle fades, glowing buttons, dreamy transitions  
- 🕯️ **Confession Mode (Customizable)** — transform the vault intro into a message of love  
- 💌 **Responsive Layout** — optimized for phone and desktop  
- 🎵 **Optional Background Music** — loop soft instrumentals or love notes
